## §Misses — diagnosis

### NYU sub-THz ASA NLOS bootstrap CFI width (Paper 69.40, Py 36.20)

The paper's bootstrap CFI on lognormal mean AS at sub-THz NLOS (n=11) reports a 69° width vs our 36°. Two likely drivers: (1) the paper may bootstrap the lognormal `μ`/`σ` jointly (not just `E[X]`) inflating the width via log-space tails; (2) the paper may sample the raw AS values with a different random seed. We match the same formula (`E[X] = exp(μ ln10 + (σ ln10)²/2)`) within sample mean and std dev — the systematic narrowing on NYU-only NLOS at small n (11) is consistent with the bootstrap underestimating tail behavior when a single high-AS location dominates the sample. Impact on conclusions: none — the reported pooled-vs-individual CFI shrinkage direction matches (Pooled 18.85° Py vs 26.42° Paper; both much smaller than either individual sample's CFI).

### 6.75 GHz NYU NLOS σ_SF (Paper 6.51, Py 7.72)

The difference likely reflects the 2 outage rows in the NYU 6.75 GHz NLOS data. The paper may exclude them from the σ_SF computation; our loader drops them where PL is NaN but the count-13 → count-11 transition is inconsistent with the paper's reported n=13. This changes the residual std. Not material to the paper's conclusions.

### 6.75 GHz Table 6 RMSE for USC PL (Paper 6.2, Py 2.7)

Mirror of the `source-data drift` issue in `issues_log.md`: the `Omni PL USC orig. (U1)` column of `7_UMi_U3.xlsx` carries slightly updated USC PL values vs. the numbers used when Table 6 was originally computed. The small-absolute-magnitude (<1 dB) differences per row compound into a 3-dB-RMSE shift. Replicating the exact Table 6 value would require accessing the superseded raw USC output; the paper's qualitative message (USC-matched-threshold RMSE is low) is preserved.
