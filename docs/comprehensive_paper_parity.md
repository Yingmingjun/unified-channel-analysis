Every miss is an AS CFI width on a small-n sample. Bootstrap `bootstrp()` in MATLAB (paper) draws different resample indices from `randi` in our MATLAB port and `default_rng` in Python, even at identical seeds. On samples of n = 11–13 with one dominant high-AS location, the 95 % tail varies by up to 2× depending on how often that location is included in a resample. This is RNG-implementation-dependent and not a scientific difference.
